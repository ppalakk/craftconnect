# 🌿 CraftConnect

### AI-Driven Market Linkage & Smart Cataloging for Marginalized Artisans

CraftConnect is a  AI platform designed to help marginalized artisans digitally showcase their products, create professional catalogs, discover suitable markets, and make better pricing and business decisions.

### 🌐 Live Demo
Experience the live application here: https://craftconnect-ekvw.onrender.com

## 🎯 Problem

Artisans often face:

* Limited digital presence
* Difficulty creating professional product catalogs
* Limited access to buyers and markets
* Lack of pricing and demand insights

## 💡 Solution

**Product Image → AI Catalog → Price Insights → Market Matching → Business Opportunities**

### ✨ Key Features

* 🤖 AI-powered smart product cataloging
* 🎯 Market & buyer recommendations
* 🎯regional language / voice input
* 🎯 offline/low-connectivity indicator
* 💰 AI-assisted price estimation
* 📊authenticity/GI-tag verification
* 📈 Demand & trend insights
* 📊 Artisan business dashboard

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

**Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
**Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
**Database:** MongoDB
**AI/ML:** Python
