# 🌿 CraftConnect

### AI-Driven Market Linkage & Smart Cataloging for Marginalized Artisans

CraftConnect is an AI platform designed to help marginalized artisans digitally showcase their products, create professional catalogs, discover suitable markets, and make better pricing and business decisions.

### 🌐 Live Demo
Experience the live application here: https://craftconnect-ekvw.onrender.com

---

## 🎯 Problem

Artisans often face:

* Limited digital presence
* Difficulty creating professional product catalogs
* Limited access to buyers and markets
* Lack of pricing and demand insights

## 💡 Solution

**Product Image → AI Catalog → Price Insights → Market Matching → Business Opportunities**

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 🤖 AI-powered smart product cataloging (Google Gemini Vision)
* 📊 Real handicraft pricing benchmarks (TRIFED, Tribes India, Cottage Emporium datasets)
* 🎯 Market & buyer recommendations (local fairs, government exhibitions, online platforms)
* 🗣️ Regional language / voice input (Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, English)
* 💰 AI-assisted price estimation grounded in 150+ authentic listings
* 📈 Demand & trend insights (real market intelligence, no fabricated data)
* 📊 Artisan business dashboard (finance P&L, expense tracking, order dispatch)
* 📴 Offline-capable with JSON file store fallback
